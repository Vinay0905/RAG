Short example
Title: Mobile Login Redesign.

Goal: Reduce login drop-offs.

Users: Existing app users.

Requirements: Email login, OTP login, forgot password.

Out of scope: Social login.

Success metric: 20% fewer failed login attempts.

Milestone: Design, development, QA, release.
